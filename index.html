👋 About Me

Hi, I'm Ali (sher) Abdukarim, a Senior Software Engineer and Site Reliability Engineer at Box, passionate about building infrastructure and applications that scale with confidence.

With nearly a decade of experience across startups and enterprise platforms, I specialize in multi-cloud infrastructure, automation tooling, and developer experience. My journey spans across engineering disciplines—from designing high-availability systems to leading cross-cloud initiatives with GCP, AWS, Azure, and IBM Cloud.

As an SRE, I've built and maintained secure Infrastructure as Code (IaC) pipelines, authored robust Terraform/Terragrunt modules, and implemented next-gen end-to-end testing frameworks tightly coupled with CI/CD workflows. I love clean abstractions, developer-first tooling, and empowering teams to ship safely and confidently.

On the dev side, I bring full stack experience using Python, Django, React, TypeScript, and Docker/Kubernetes to ship impactful software. Whether it's building a feature-rich API or polishing UI interactions—I enjoy solving real-world problems at scale.

Certified by Google Cloud, AWS, HashiCorp, Docker, and Red Hat, I continually strive to grow through learning, mentoring, and building in public.
